# ASO and launch workstream

Status: project-specific plan, 4 November 2026. Keyword volume, competitiveness and listing conversion have not been measured.

## Initial brief

Audience hypothesis: people who want everyday habit tracking and private unwanted-habit tracking in one place, with quick widget check-ins and a consistent record across iOS and Android.

Promise: build better habits, break unwanted ones, and see progress across devices.

First value event: create a habit and record the first action. Repeat value: a daily check-in, including widget-only use. Paid value: unlimited tracking, richer statistics and cross-device continuity.

Start research with US English; review India pricing and English search language as a secondary market. Final release territories remain open.

## Tasks that can begin before the app is complete

- Collect authentic search/review language for three intents: habit tracking, mobile widgets and cross-platform tracking, and quitting unwanted habits.
- Compare the first search results and review complaints for each intent. Record storefront, date and evidence, separating observed data from research-tool estimates.
- Shortlist an original brand and check naming availability before making final assets.
- Draft one clear default store promise and a separate quit-focused message. Avoid making the whole product look like a porn-specific app if the general workflow remains the main audience.
- Keep candidate keywords in `marketing/` with source/date/status. Do not buy research subscriptions just to create a first candidate list.

## Assets once the corresponding screens work

1. Today: build and quit habits together.
2. The small single-habit widget: show exactly the shipping size/layout.
3. Progress: grid, streaks and useful quit statistics.
4. Urge support: personal reason and short replacement action.
5. Cross-platform continuity: the same record on iOS and Android, only once verified on both.
6. Privacy: discreet widget labels and account-free local use.

Use real app captures with fictional data. Prepare iPhone App Store assets first, then Android-specific Play assets, description fields and accurate upgrade copy. Do not advertise a full Mac app or use Mac screenshots for the mobile launch. Capture video only after the main interaction is polished.

Check limits and submission requirements when preparing the final listing; do not treat today's guidance as frozen release rules. [Apple product-page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/).

## Experiments after beta learning

Start with the general habit-tracker page on the App Store, then adapt the positioning and store fields for Google Play rather than copying Apple metadata mechanically. Consider a quit-focused iPhone custom product page using the same app, with messaging matching the delivered experience. [Apple custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/).

Test a widget-led versus progress-led first screenshot when traffic supports a useful result. Compare activation and retained check-ins alongside listing conversion. For pricing, compare net receipts per activated install and renewal/retention, not just the share choosing an annual plan.

Store screenshots may only promise implemented behaviour. The plan does not assume ASO will generate enough traffic or that a competitor's pricing explains its success.
