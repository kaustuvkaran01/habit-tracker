Tell us what happened, what you expected and, if useful, your app version and device model. Please omit private habit details, notes and backup files unless you deliberately choose to share them. The app does not attach them to support messages automatically. You review and send the email yourself.

## Recover an archived habit

Open Settings → Archived Habits and restore the habit. Free archives have a 30-day recovery window. Pro retains archives while Pro access is verified; after Pro ends, protected archives receive a fresh 30-day recovery window. Earned collections can remain after ordinary habit deletion.

## Back up or restore your tracker

Open Settings → Backup & Restore. Export a backup and check that it was saved in your chosen destination. Choose a backup file to preview its contents. Add missing habits preserves existing copies; Replace local habits replaces the whole habit/collection snapshot after confirmation. Export first if you want to keep the current version.

Backups include private notes and are not encrypted by the app. Export/import is Free. Extra imported habits stay saved under Free's four-habit logging allowance. Imported reminder times start paused; review and enable them in the habit editor. Invalid files or failed restores have retry/recovery paths. A lost local tracker cannot be recovered by the developer without a backup you retained.

## Reminder help

Save an enabled reminder and allow notifications. If access is denied, use the device-settings action in reminder status. Check the selected weekdays, time and whether the habit is already complete. Opening the app refreshes its finite queue of future reminders. Review reminder times after timezone travel. Discreet text is enabled by default. If a reminder was restored from a file, explicitly enable it after review.

## Correct an entry or add a note

Open a habit and hold a calendar day to open its editor. Screen-reader users can use the cell's Edit entry action. Notes must be enabled in General settings to appear. A failed save retains a retry route; retry saves the result without applying a numeric increment again.

## Erase local app data

Open Settings → Backup & Restore → Erase all local data and follow the typed confirmation. This removes local habits, notes, rewards, archives and preferences, including retained collections. External exported copies and device backups are not erased by this action. Export a backup first if you may need the history later. Cleanup retries after interruption before showing habit data.

## Privacy

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md) or Settings → Privacy Policy. In-app cloud sync and purchases are not connected in the current development build; plan selection does not charge money. Release support information will be updated when these services are connected.
