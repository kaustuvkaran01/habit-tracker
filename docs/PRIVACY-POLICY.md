# Habit Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes the current Habit Tracker app. Habit Tracker is the working name used during development. Contact the developer at [kaustuvkaran@gmail.com](mailto:kaustuvkaran@gmail.com) for privacy or support questions.

## Data on your device

Habit Tracker saves habit names and descriptions, icons, categories, schedules, tracking rules, entries and corrections, notes, recording dates/timezone context, archives and earned rewards on your device. App preferences include appearance, language, haptics, dashboard choices and reminder settings. No app account is required.

The app does not send your habit history or notes to a developer-operated server. Current app code has no connected advertising, user-tracking or crash-reporting service. Charts and achievement calculations run locally. App records are not separately encrypted by this app; device security and operating-system backup settings apply. Device backups may include app data. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local records.

## Notifications

With your permission, reminders are scheduled locally through your device's notification service. Default reminder content is discreet. You may explicitly choose to display a habit name or custom daily-review text. Notes are never included in reminder content. You control notification access in device settings. This app does not register for or send habit data through a remote push-notification service in the current version.

## Sharing, wallpapers and backups

You choose when to share a habit or badge image or export a wallpaper or backup file. Habit sharing can include the description and progress information you select in its preview. Exported JSON backups include habit history and private notes and are not encrypted by the app. Save them only somewhere you trust.

The destination or service you select handles the exported copy under its own practices. Owned temporary export files and tracked import cache copies are cleaned up after normal completion; an interrupted operation may leave temporary files until cleanup runs again. The app does not delete copies you saved or sent outside its storage. It cannot confirm that every destination saved a file merely because a share sheet closed.

Restore validates and previews files before you confirm adding missing habits or replacing local habit data. Device preferences and notification permission remain on the device. Imported reminder times start paused for review. Restore does not grant paid access or replay historical achievement celebrations.

## Archiving and erasing data

Deleting a habit first archives it. Free archives have a 30-day recovery period; Pro archives are retained while access is verified as Pro. After Pro ends, protected archives receive a fresh 30-day recovery window. Automatic cleanup pauses when access cannot be verified. Cleanup runs when the app can process its stored data; this is not a promise of background deletion at an exact time.

Ordinary archive expiry/deletion can preserve earned collection snapshots, including the habit name, icon, color and earned wallpapers/badges. This keeps achievements available independently of active habit tracking.

Settings → Backup & Restore → Erase all local data is the separate option to remove local habits, notes, archives, retained collections, rewards and app preferences. It requires confirmation. The app removes its own notification jobs and owned temporary files and resumes interrupted cleanup before loading habit data. External exported copies and operating-system backups remain under your control. OS notification permission is not revoked by local erasure.

## Support messages

The Support screen opens your email app with a generic subject. It does not attach habit history, notes, logs, identifiers or a backup. You decide whether to send the message and what to include. Your email provider handles delivery. If you email the developer, the developer receives your address and the content you send to respond to your request. Avoid including sensitive habit details; contact the same address if you need help removing support correspondence.

## App-switcher cover and device security

The current app displays a cover when its interface becomes inactive or backgrounded, including app-owned drawers and screens. It is a visual privacy aid, not authentication or encryption. It does not block screenshots, screen recording or access by someone using an unlocked device. Device-level snapshot timing still requires native-build verification; do not rely on the development preview as a security guarantee. Face ID/device-lock protection is not included in this version.

## Platform services and future changes

Expo Go is a separate development host, and Apple, Google, device backups, email and chosen share destinations have their own privacy practices. This policy does not describe data those providers handle independently of the app. In-app iCloud sync, app accounts and store purchases are not connected in the current build. Their data flows and any new diagnostic service will be reviewed and this policy updated before integration is released.
